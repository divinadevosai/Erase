Erase

Companies you've never heard of buy and sell your name, address and habits. The law says they must stop using it for marketing the moment you object, and delete it unless they have a legal reason to keep it. Erase writes the letters, opens them in your email, and keeps track of who still owes you a reply.

Your details stay in this browser. This page is blocked from making any network requests, so it can't send your details anywhere. The only thing that leaves is the email you choose to send. On a shared computer, press Clear all my data when you're done.
